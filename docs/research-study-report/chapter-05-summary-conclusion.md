# Chapter Five: Summary, Conclusion and Recommendations

## 5.1 Summary
This study focused on the design and implementation of the Seminary Management and Results System to improve academic and administrative operations. The system addressed key institutional needs in student records management, course registration, result handling, transcript generation, finance tracking, and reporting.

## 5.2 Conclusion
The study concludes that a well-designed computerized management and results system can significantly improve efficiency, accuracy, accessibility of information, and service delivery within an educational institution. SMNS provides a practical solution for managing academic and finance-related workflows in a centralized environment.

## 5.3 Recommendations
- Expand integration with external platforms where needed.
- Continue improving usability for all user roles.
- Strengthen analytics and reporting features.
- Add more automation for approval workflows and notifications.
- Maintain regular backups, security reviews, and user training.

## 5.4 Limitations of the Study
- The study is based on the currently implemented modules.
- Some advanced integrations may still be pending.
- The evaluation may depend on the local institutional environment.

## 5.5 Areas for Future Work
- Mobile-friendly extensions
- Broader systems integration
- Advanced auditing and analytics
- Enhanced self-service workflows

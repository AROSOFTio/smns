# SOP: Operations Runbook and Staff Training

## 1. Purpose

Define day-to-day operational procedures for staff and a repeatable training process to ensure users perform duties consistently and safely.

## 2. Scope

- Admin operations
- Academic operations
- Finance operations
- Service desk and support-facing procedures

## 3. Required Operational SOP Coverage

Each module owner must maintain written procedures for:

1. Start-of-day checks (access status, queue backlog, critical alerts)
2. Core daily tasks (module-specific workflows)
3. End-of-day checks (exceptions, unresolved actions, pending approvals)
4. Escalation triggers and ownership
5. Evidence/log updates

## 4. Training Process

1. Training plan is prepared per role (`admin`, `academic`, `finance`, `lecturer`, `student support`).
2. Initial onboarding training is mandatory before production access.
3. Refresher training is performed at least quarterly or after major workflow changes.
4. Training completion is recorded in `templates/User-Training-Register.csv`.
5. Failed assessments or repeated errors require targeted retraining and manager sign-off.

## 5. Training Content Minimums

- Role boundaries and separation-of-duties expectations
- Security practices (password/MFA/session handling)
- Approved workflow steps and prohibited shortcuts
- Incident reporting and escalation path
- Audit evidence expectations

## 6. Evidence to Retain

- Attendance/completion records
- Assessment results (if used)
- Version of materials delivered
- Trainer and approver details

## 7. Review Cadence

- Monthly: training completion review for active staff
- Quarterly: refresher schedule review
- Annually: SOP and material update
